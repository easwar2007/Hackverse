Can you reconcile the distinction between internal evidence and cross-epigraphic correlation in Chola copper plates?

FM Faculty Mentor (Lexicon Mode)

In Chola epigraphy, copper-plate grants (śāsana) exhibit a structured dichotomy. The prasasti (eulogy) relies on genealogical poetic hyperbole, whereas the operative section (vyavasthā) mandates rigid cadastral survey verification.

edit_note Marginal Note § 4.12 Karandai Plates, r. 1020 CE

Cross-epigraphic validation occurs when regional stone inscriptions at Thanjavur Brihadisvara verify identical taxable land demarcations (kāṇi) and water sluice allocations.
